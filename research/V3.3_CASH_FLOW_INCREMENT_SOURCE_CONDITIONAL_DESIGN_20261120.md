# V3.3 — Cash-flow information increment: fixed, source-conditional 20-session research design

**Recorded:** 2026-11-20, **before any new outcome fetch, cohort construction or strategy performance run in this batch**.  
**Status:** `DESIGN_FROZEN_FOR_SOURCE_ADMISSION_ONLY / NOT_A_REGISTERED_CLEAN_OOS_TEST / NO VERIFIED ALPHA / NO BUY`. This document **does not certify** that historic data periods are unseen: prior project research has opened several historical windows. No outcome, performance, sign or threshold is inferred here.

## 1. Economic question

Does a newly disclosed improvement in **year-to-date operating cash flow per share**, relative to the same reporting period one year earlier, contain incremental information about an A share's *next 20 eligible trading sessions' after-cost executable excess return* **beyond** revenue growth already disclosed?

Mechanism: a cash-flow improvement *might* identify greater cash realization or reduced short-term funding pressure, but timing, changes in working capital, mergers, equity base and seasonality can reverse this interpretation. This is a two-sided falsifiable hypothesis, **not a positive-return assumption**. No requirement that historical share price rose, and no hard gate that revenue or profits must have grown.

The archive's `ann_date` must be reconciled against original filed dates and revisions. **Only a first eligible trading session strictly later than the proven disclosure date may become a decision session**; any publication-order ambiguity defaults to a later session rather than an earlier trade.

## 3. Fixed feature design (no outcome-dependent sign selection)

**Observation:** fiscal Q3 year-to-date values only (Jan–Sep); never compare a Q3 nine-month cash-flow level with Q1 or annual figures. Let `CFOps_y` = original-as-of Q3 `ocfps` for that company's year y and `CFOps_y-1` the analogous previous year's 9-month value, as each was observable at signal time.

- Primary scalar signal: `delta_CFOps = CFOps_y - CFOps_y-1` in CNY per share (same report duration).
- Equal-sized within-sector/date **ranks** of `delta_CFOps` are compared with a model using `or_yoy` alone; sector, market cap and liquidity should be used as pre-signal controls only if original-vintage coverage is demonstrably available. Do not silently replace missing controls with future data.
- **Model increment**: a fixed simple linear/rank score with `or_yoy` plus `delta_CFOps` versus the same procedure with `or_yoy` alone. No post-outcome hyperparameter search, no arbitrary growth thresholds, no cherry-picking positive-only revisions. Industry/size neutralization is contingent on genuinely historical metadata.
- A cash-flow measure can move for reasons unrelated to recurring operations; the first source quality review must explicitly assess share-count changes, restatements, extraordinary receipts and cases with different accounting meaning. If `ocfps` source semantics differ across years, **the signal is not admitted**. This is a risk test, not a license to manually exclude losing names.

## 4. Economic experiment contract (cannot run until source gates pass)

- **Universe:** full contemporaneously listed and tradable Shanghai + Shenzhen A shares, including rejected/non-signaling names and delistings as the dated source allows. Reconstruct listed date, ST/suspension/trading permission, board and minimum lot at each decision time. Fail source admission when this universe cannot be reconciled, **not** merely when a few stocks are missing.
- **Event decision date:** strictly after each Q3 disclosure and the comparable prior-year Q3 record is already public. Never place entry at a close whose time precedes disclosure.
- **Return:** entry from the earliest realistically executable *later* session (e.g., next eligible opening with limit/suspension safeguards) to the 20th exchange-session endpoint per original common calendar. Track no fills, lot affordability, at least 100 shares (STAR 200 minimum), commission minimums, stamp tax where applicable, slippage, cash, and time-matched **total-return** benchmark. These must be defined before actual evaluation; unknown price limits or tick execution are **not** assumed to fill.
- **Comparison:** same event-date/sector eligible non-signal controls or a matched market/sector benchmark, **and** an identical model excluding `delta_CFOps`. The primary claim is positive *incremental* net benchmark-relative performance out of sample, not an in-sample t-statistic or a short-term MSE.
- **Independence:** 2023–24 low-vol/reversal/momentum outcomes, 2025 growth-quality outcomes, 2018–20 growth-quality tests, 2026 SSE margin opened date, and the protected 2026 122/8 episode **must not** be relabeled unseen. Candidate earlier years (e.g., 2011–2017) are **not presumed clean** just because they are old. Audit full repository outcome-read history and source data release vintage **before** naming untouched train/holdout years; if no qualifying independent period exists, classify as descriptive development and do not claim OOS. The frozen 122/8 / E-layer never become this test's sandbox.
- **No target movement:** The relevant economic target remains **20 exchange sessions**, net of costs, realistic fill and benchmark-relative. One-day MSE or unadjusted raw returns cannot be substituted for it.

## 6. Why this is not repeating a failed experiment

The closed 2018–20 growth/ROE and 2025 growth-quality experiments selected mainly on profitability/growth; they did not establish an independent incremental *cash-flow-change vs revenue-only* test. This design has a distinct economic question and no historical price-rise condition. **But** that difference alone does not make its subsequent results independent. Avoid the closed outcomes and preserve their failures.

No additional GitHub Actions workflow, scheduler, model package or trading API is required to express this admission decision. Existing validity, economics and discipline engines remain the intended downstream evaluators once a genuinely admissible cohort exists.
