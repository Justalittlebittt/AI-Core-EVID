### Mechanism checks and key questions

**Operations with accounting cash-flow lag — 沪电股份, 深南电路.** Both disclosed robust 2026 YTD deducted-profit growth, but operating CFO is only ~0.49 and ~0.48 times deducted parent profit. Their company Q3 reports disclose YTD CFO growth of **-6.61%** and **-15.36%** respectively. Before treating these as durable growth opportunities, examine receivables collection, inventory turnover, working-capital change, payment terms, capacity ramps and capex financing. The same-period CFO/core ratio is an observational accounting cross-check, not free cash flow or a trained investability threshold.

**Stronger YTD cash-flow accompaniment — 睿创微纳, 三环集团.** CFO/core ratios ~1.11 and ~0.93. Their reported operating CFO growth is **+224.85%** and **+72.18%**. The next work is to verify if sales collections, inventory, orders, customer concentration and capex requirements sustain that cash performance, and determine realistic valuation; strong cash numbers do not prove an attractive entry price.

**Business-cycle comparators — 中国巨石, 神火股份.** Their CFO/core ratios ~0.74 and ~1.48. 中国巨石 says third-quarter profit growth came from increased volume and price; 神火股份 is exposed to industrial metal/coal price cycles. Their high deducted profit may reflect a price cycle rather than stable recurring investment alpha. Compare commodity-price-normalized profitability before valuation.

An added market warning: third-party daily tables show 沪电股份 closing 2026-10-29 at ¥94.11, -10% that session, illustrating that a good earnings report is not itself a safe BUY trigger. This historical price observation is **not** an out-of-sample strategy evaluation and is **not** a live quote.
