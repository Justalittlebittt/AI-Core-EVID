## Cost, corporate actions and market-price vintage

- BaoStock daily **unadjusted** `adjustflag=3` close-to-close price return is the primary descriptive number, captured in 2026. An additional **2026-vintage forward-adjusted** `adjustflag=2` comparison yielded the same price-return figures across these two short windows to display precision, but **does not independently verify** the economic total return, cash dividends/splits, exchange-adjusted prices, accurate tradeability at close, or historically authentic same-day price vintage.
- The **30 basis point round-trip deduction is a hypothetical assumption**, not an executed cost record. There is no actual fill, spread, stamp duty treatment by ticket, realistic limit-up/limit-down execution, or tax record. Entry and exit sessions showed `tradestatus=1` and not ST at reference close, but this is **not** proof one could transact the closing auction price.
- Index values were matched to **each security's own two dates**, preventing the earlier class of cross-vintage return-endpoint mistakes.
- No PE/PB historical value is promoted as genuinely observed contemporaneously in 2025: current historical vendor snapshots can include subsequent denominator updates.
