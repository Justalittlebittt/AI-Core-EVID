## Four causal/valuation questions, not mechanical thresholds

**三环集团:** Reported order demand and some volume/price growth align with cash generation; CFO/core-profit ~0.93. However accounts receivable +75.91% from end-2025; construction in progress +234%; reported period-end cash grew heavily due to **H-share listing proceeds**, not recurring operating cash. Need segment, collection, project cash commitments and diluted share-count valuation. [Company Q3 report](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12670181&stockid=300408).

**睿创微纳:** CFO/core-profit ~1.11 and 18.93 亿元 CFO less capex are encouraging observations; company reports strong in-hand/new orders and production capacity ramp. Those orders are company assertions, not independently authenticated customer contracts. Inventory rose 46.28% vs year-end; investigate delivery, inventory aging, customer concentration, R&D and future spend. Cash balance dynamics are affected by non-operating investment and financing flows. [Company Q3 report](https://vip.stock.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12661732&stockid=688002) and [cash-flow statement](https://www.cfi.net.cn/p20261026003240.html).

**沪电股份:** CFO/core-profit ~0.49, CFO YoY −6.61%; 52.77 亿元 capex payment greatly exceeds 27.04 亿元 CFO. Need plant ramp yields, cash collections, investment funding, dilution and incremental capital return; negative proxy alone does not imply failure. [Q3 report](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12664696&stockid=002463).

**深南电路:** CFO/core-profit ~0.48, CFO YoY −15.36%; 76.13 亿元 capex vs 19.59 亿元 CFO. Reported receivable/inventory impairments, construction in progress and new borrowing increase capital-recovery and funding questions. Need future plant utilization/receivables, debt costs and liquidity. [Q3 report](https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12655043&stockid=002916) and [cash-flow details](https://cfi.net.cn/p20261022002319.html).
