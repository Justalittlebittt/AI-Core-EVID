## Problem elimination decision — quality-growth historical study

**Stop adding repeated issuer-PDF probes to the 2021/2022 research lane.** Four missing SSE PDFs and one ambiguous identity are no longer the dominant blockers. **Even perfect authentication of all selected 20 issuer reports would NOT establish a PIT-selected, survivorship-free top-10 strategy**, because its rank ordering and selection denominator also depend on the **entire thousands-name as-of universe** and historical versions of *nonselected* issuers (including delisted/ST and past corrections). Authenticating the winners alone cannot correct retrospective selection leakage.

Separate unresolved requirements:
1. cross-sectional source-vintage and listing/ST identity for the **whole eligibility population**; no current independently proven 2021/22 historical full-universe original financial source.
2. Historical actual open auction fill under limit-up and halt restrictions, exact 100-share lot cost/cash mechanics and corporate-action cashflows.
3. The true 2021/22 official CSI300 TR `H00300` matched session values, currently unqueried as part of the sealed evaluation design.

Therefore classify the fixed V3.1 quality-growth 2021/2022 *tradable-alpha validation* as:
`EVIDENCE_BLOCKED_UNDER_CURRENT_FREE_SOURCE_PROVENANCE / NO_VERIFIED_ALPHA / NO_BUY`.

This does **not** assert the signal loses money, and does **not** invalidate the 20 observed provisional financial candidates. It marks an insufficient evidence route for **validated excess return**. Do not upgrade it to positive/negative alpha; do not quietly replace its benchmark, dates, members or frozen requirements.

**Recommended shortest mainline continuation:** shift to the already declared 2018–2020 **development-only** years to exercise the predeclared growth/ROE end-to-end after-cost economic-return path on exploratory observations if free data permits (explicitly `DEV_NOT_OOS`, not an independent proof).
