## Boundary, reproducibility, and methods

The issuer/period selection was fixed **before looking at historical stock outcomes** by `scripts/v31_qlib_pdf_crosscheck_inputs.py`, from the previously hash-selected 24 issuers and vendor listing eligibility. One-shot workflow https://github.com/Justalittlebittt/AI-Core-EVID/actions/runs/44030779227 logged `PRESELECTED_PDF_VERIFICATION_INPUTS`. Two issuers are repeated across 2018, 2020, and 2022 Q3: `600532.SH`, `600006.SH`. This is six issuer-periods, **not** six independent companies or a representative full-market test. Some prior plan language requested multi-sector original documents; this fixed selection yields only two issuers and cannot satisfy multi-sector breadth.

This document reconciles **three source-text-observable fields per issuer-period**: revenue YoY % (`or_yoy`), weighted ROE % (`roe_waa`), and cumulative non-recurring-items-excluded attributable profit in RMB (`profit_dedt`). Source decimal percentages rounded to the two-digit issuer presentation; the profit amounts are exact. `ocfps` is **not authenticated here** because precise period-share denominator and company-specific per-share definition require separate source verification. Original-PDF byte hashes and page-by-page visual authentication were not obtained; web PDF screenshot attempts timed out. The historical announcement text presented on third-party mirrored archival pages was checked, not a certified 2018/2020/2022 archived original PDF binary.
