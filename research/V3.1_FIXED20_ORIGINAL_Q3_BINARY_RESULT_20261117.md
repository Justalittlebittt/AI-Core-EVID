The result does **not** certify that 15 or 16 financial rows were semantically authenticated, nor guarantee the source PDF was byte-for-byte identical to the document at 2021/2022 market decision time. `002932.SZ` 2021 original PDF identity cannot be silently promoted because all 3 tokens were seen.
