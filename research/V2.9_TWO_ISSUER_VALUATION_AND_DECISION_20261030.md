## Valuation sensitivity: explicit what-if arithmetic, not an estimated price range

**Identity:** hypothetical 12-month per-share price = **observed price × (1 + change in trailing EPS) × (terminal PE / observed PE)**. This deliberately simplified no-dividend, no-fee, fixed-share-count identity is *not* discounted cash flow, a price prediction, a backtest, predeclared alpha or a fair-value estimate. In particular the hypothetical change in trailing EPS is **not** the recently reported historical +76–82% revenue growth.

Three transparent, **illustrative and uncalibrated** joint conditions:

| Illustration | Future trailing EPS change | PE multiple relative to 2026-10-29 | Implied simple price change | 三环集团 hypothetical A-share price | 睿创微纳 hypothetical A-share price |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Earnings contraction with PE compression | −10% | 65% of anchor (−35%) | **−41.5%** | ¥49.46 | ¥81.33 |
| EPS rises but valuation compresses | +20% | 80% of anchor (−20%) | **−4.0%** | ¥81.16 | ¥133.47 |
| EPS rises and the multiple holds | +40% | 100% of anchor | **+40.0%** | ¥118.36 | ¥194.64 |

All 3×3 combinations of hypothetical EPS changes (−10%, +20%, +40%) and PE retention (65%, 80%, 100%) are in [the machine-readable calculation](V2.9_ISSUER_VALUATION_SENSITIVITY_20261030.json), with input quote identities and sources. **The endpoints are not real market quotes, price targets, expected outcomes or probabilistically weighted scenarios.** These grid assumptions are chosen for stress visualization alone; there is **no empirical support** for either issuer earning those EPS growth rates or trading on those terminal PE ratios.

**Break-even insight:** a **20% PE contraction requires +25% growth in trailing EPS** just to hold the *same* price, before dividends, fees and spread. Thus an earnings-growth headline can coexist with a negative stock return.

The actual exact terminal PE to be justified for each company's industry, normalized earnings, capital structure, dilution and risk premium is **not established**. In particular no cross-company valuation ranking should be inferred from 23.82x vs 37.30x alone: products, earnings mix and A/H capitalization differ.

## Concrete research decision and disconfirming evidence

**睿创微纳 — PRIORITY_FUNDAMENTAL_VALIDATION, NO_ACTION:** confirm component/product shipment growth and signed order conversion rather than simply management's order optimism, disaggregate production-capacity ramp vs price/mix, inventory aging and working-capital collections, examine convertible/diluted share count and normalized gross margin. A breakdown in order delivery, declining cash collections or earnings dilution materially larger than forecast will challenge the growth thesis.

**三环集团 — PRIORITY_FUNDAMENTAL_VALIDATION, NO_ACTION:** reconcile issued H shares with period-end share count and A/H market valuation, separate listing proceeds from operating cash, investigate high receivables, construction-in-progress project returns, order backlog duration and whether gross-margin uplift reflects cyclical volume/price. Deteriorating collections or weak new project returns would challenge the thesis.

**No stock has an authorized entry price, stop/take-profit or position size**, since such trade precommitments require a buy-eligible strategy and user risk constraints; inventing them would be false precision. Under the existing V2.9 validity contract, the next causal proof is to test a frozen financial hypothesis against independent, later observations with a same-date market/sector benchmark, trading costs, true point-in-time availability, and drawdown/regime checks. Do not recycle already-inspected 2026-Q3 records or 2026-10-29 price into a claimed pristine OOS test. Do not modify protected V2.8.12 E-layer and Fold3/Fold4.
